       ↓
BUY some bonds
       ↓

The chapter explains that rebalancing restores the desired allocation.

86. The Most Important Concepts to Remember

If you're learning quantitative trading from the beginning, don't try to memorize every word yet.

Focus on this:

1. DATA
   ↓
   Price, volume, news, fundamentals

2. FEATURES
   ↓
   Useful information extracted from data

3. MODEL
   ↓
   Turns features into prediction

4. SIGNAL
   ↓
   BUY / SELL / HOLD

5. BACKTEST
   ↓
   Test strategy on historical data

6. RISK
   ↓
   Prevent large losses

7. EXECUTION
   ↓
   Actually place orders

8. PERFORMANCE
   ↓
   Profit + risk + costs

And the most important distinction:

QUANT TRADING
                   │
       ┌───────────┴───────────┐
       ▼                       ▼
   FIND SIGNALS           EXECUTE ORDERS
       │                       │
       ▼                       ▼
 "Should I buy?"         "How do I buy?"

Signal generation and order execution are related but different problems.

The chapter's overall progression is essentially: understand quantitative trading → understand markets/instruments → understand trading mechanics → obtain financial data → analyze and visualize it → eventually build trading strategies.
