## Define research report. Explain structure of writing research report

## Research report
> A research report is description of things that have been done during the process of research.

> It is written in last stage of research.

> The research report provide information to concern parties regarding analysis & findings of research work.

## Structure of Research Report
1 Preliminary Part
> Before writing body of research few pages of preliminary materials are included.

a Title page
> First page of research report is title page. It contains:
-  Title of study
- Full name of candidate
- Name of institution to which report is submitted
- Degree for which report is presented
- Name and year when degree is to be conferred

b Acceptance page
> It includes advisor's signature along with title and name of researcher.

> Examiner's name should also be included.

c Acknowledgement page
> It includes statement of purpose, scope and organization of report.

> In this page researcher also express thanks to those who help him in completing his research activities.

d Table of contents
> It Include chapters included in research report.

> The heading 'Table of contents' must be written in capital letters at center of page.

> With each chapter page number is also included along with sub-headings.

e List of tables / figures
> In new page after table of contents, table and figures are listed.

2 The main body
> It is the main part of research report which contains:

a Introduction
> It includes purpose of study, problem, theoretical framework, research hypothesis, definition of terms used in study etc.
